FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q1.

Who can join CIPHER?

expand_more
Any MITAOE student with an urge to build. We don't gatekeep by branch or GPA. Show us what you've broken, what repositories you've starred, or what ambitious architecture you want to construct with us.
Q2.

Is Velora only for advanced hackers?

expand_more
Zero prerequisites except curiosity and stamina. First-year students have previously won cash bounties with scrappy, inventive MVPs over over-engineered repositories. We supply mentorship rounds throughout the night.
Q3.

How many hours do council members put in?

expand_more
Flexible during normal academic cycles, but during hackathon release weeks it turns into an exhilarating war-room bunker experience with free catering, late-night testing sprints, and infinite laughs.
Q4.

Can I participate if I am not in the IT branch?

expand_more
100% yes. If you write code, train models, solder microcontrollers, or craft digital interfaces, you belong in our arena. Technical merit is agnostic to departmental paperwork.
